## Activation requirements

- Store short recognizable monophonic phrases as semitone offsets and beat
  durations, with source, composer, title aliases, and excerpt location.
- Verify melody AND rhythm against the source; check octave direction and rests.
- Keep the keytar synth; no recording downloads are needed for playback.
- Rotate the full playable collection without replacement; present 4 choices
  for early levels and 6 for advanced levels, always including the target.
- Source/license metadata must cover the actual supplied notation data.
- Pending titles must not enter quizzes until their audio data is ready.
