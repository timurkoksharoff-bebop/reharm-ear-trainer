# Greg Sullivan Wurlitzer EP200

- Instrument and samples: Greg Sullivan
- SFZ mapping: kinwie
- License: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported (CC BY 3.0)
- Original source: https://github.com/sfzinstruments/GregSullivan.E-Pianos
- Web conversion source: https://github.com/danigb/samples

The audio files in this directory are sampled recordings of a Wurlitzer EP200
electric piano. They were converted from the original FLAC distribution to AAC
in an M4A container for web playback and are redistributed under CC BY 3.0.
