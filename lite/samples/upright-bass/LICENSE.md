# VSCO 2 Community Edition — Solo Contrabass Pizzicato

The upright-bass samples in this directory are derived from the Solo
Contrabass Pizzicato recordings in VSCO 2 Community Edition.

- Source: https://github.com/sgossner/VSCO-2-CE
- Recorded by Sam Gossner and Simon Dalzell
- Sample cutting by Elan Hickler / Soundemote
- License: CC0 1.0 Universal
- License text: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

For this application, selected source WAV files were level-matched and
converted to compact AAC/M4A files.
